A release about making your own materials and working with other tools. Every material now opens as an Unreal-style **Material Graph**, and a **Shader Graph** lets you build your own shader for Summoner's Rift ground materials. It ships to the game with your mod; this was confirmed in game. ReyEngine now reads and writes LTK Manager's layered mods, so an imported mod's game-data changes can be previewed and edited. The Character window gains a **Chroma** tab that recolours a skin, its parameters and its effects. And the Blender link is rebuilt around **Rey's Mapgeo Blender Addon**, which now carries materials, face edits, the navgrid and map visibility.

## Added

- **Material Graph.** The Material Editor's **Material Graph** button opens a node view of the material, laid out like Unreal's Material Editor. Every parameter, texture, switch and render state is a node or a Details entry. Editing there goes through the same Material Editor: the same undo, the same Save.
  - Wire, unwire, add and delete texture and parameter nodes on the shader's inputs.
  - Pins are typed: a wire the shader cannot take glows red and says why.
  - Every gesture is one undo step.
  - Materials from a linked bin open read-only, with the reason shown.
- **Shader Graph: your own shader, shipped to the game.** For `DefaultEnv_Flat`, the base shader of most Summoner's Rift ground, **New Shader Graph** builds a custom pixel shader from nodes.
  - **Nodes:** Texture Sample, Parameter, Constant, Colour, TexCoord, Time, more than 35 math and vector nodes, Panner, Rotator, and a Custom HLSL node.
  - **Templates:** Riot look, Tinted texture, Scrolling texture, Two-texture blend by mask, Pulsing emissive.
  - **Your own parameters and textures** become entries on the material.
  - **Editing gestures:**
    - Drag a wire into empty space to pick a node that connects itself.
    - Right-click an input to promote it to a parameter.
    - F2 renames a node's label.
    - Comment boxes and multi-select.
  - **Live preview:** the graph compiles locally and previews on the sphere, cube, plane or particle quad. The D3D11 map viewport draws an assigned material with its graph and updates within about two seconds of an edit.
  - **Assigning:** **Assign** in the Material Editor's SHADER GRAPH section puts a graph on a material. Export .fantome and Build Package then generate the shader cache patch and a `shaders.bin` that declares your parameters and textures.
  - **Export safety:** export refuses any state that would crash the game, naming the material and the item. These include an undeclared parameter or texture, a stray custom entry on an unassigned material, a graph with errors, and a missing default texture.
- **Chroma tab in the Character window.** It lists everything a skin draws in colour, and which other skins share it. The recolour sliders cover hue, saturation, brightness, colourise, a hue range and a grey guard. They recolour:
  - the skin's body textures, live, saved into the project;
  - its colour parameters, saved into the skin bin;
  - its effects: colour values, keys and colour textures. Effects start switched off, because most effect systems are shared with other skins.
- **LTK Manager's layered mods, in and out:**
  - **Export .fantome** writes LTK's layered format, and game-bin edits ship as GameData.
  - A forced map skin ships as a reference that LTK resolves against the installed game, so it survives patches.
  - **Import .fantome** reads layered mods, such as Crauzer's Winter Rift 2025, and keeps every layer's GameData exactly.
  - ReyEngine applies GameData the way LTK Manager does. The engine is a C# port of LTK's own, and its output matches LTK's byte for byte.
  - An imported mod shows in the viewport, the editors, the Character window and the Content Browser as LTK installs it.
  - Edits to the bins it changes are saved as declarations on top of it.
- **Rey's Mapgeo Blender Addon is ReyEngine's Blender add-on.** It replaces the old ReyEngine Bridge add-on.
  - **Installing:** Settings ▸ Blender installs it from a folder, a .zip or a GitHub release link.
  - **Requirement:** add-on v0.6.0, which speaks this release's link protocol. Older versions are refused.
  - **What the link carries:**
    - materials, textures and material assignments into Blender;
    - material edits and face edits back to ReyEngine, which stays the only program that writes your files.
  - **Face edits on instanced meshes now arrive.** The edited mesh gets its own vertex buffer, keeping its second UV, vertex colours and grass pivots.
  - **The link is protected** by a per-session code, and ignores anything that is not the add-on.
  - **The navgrid** shows in Blender the same way as in ReyEngine's overlay. It is view only.
  - **Map visibility** in Blender follows ReyEngine's rules exactly. That covers the map visibility, Baron pit and event choices, and render regions, and **Sync from ReyEngine** copies the editor's current choices.
- **Navgrid variants.** The navgrid overlay draws the grid the map really uses: the one its map container names. A **NavGrid variant** block can pick any of the map's grids, plus the dragon or Baron state overlays: Chemtech, Infernal, Mountain, Ocean, the Baron pits, and the two Atakhan sides. **Auto** follows the Map Visibility and Baron Pit choices.
- **Theme wallpapers.** Each of the eight palettes ships a matching background picture. A picture of your own is kept across palette changes.

## Improved

- **Face editing:**
  - Saving writes the mesh exactly as it looks.
  - Shift-drag adds a box of faces to the selection.
  - Face picking stays on the mesh being edited.
- **Character Arena:**
  - Casts aim at the cursor.
  - Arena spells start without a warm-up.
  - The full map content loads.
- **Autosave** runs when the editor goes quiet, and open documents are flushed every two minutes.
- **A light's icon selects it** when the map's light fit moves the light, and its gizmo sits on it.
- **Textures named only by a hashed link load** in the D3D11 map viewport. The white Worlds_Ground tiles are fixed.

## Fixed

- Closing a window while a Direct3D 11 scene was still building could crash the editor.
- A removed material parameter came back after a later save.
- A save that waited for the GameData preview now refuses if the open map changed in the meantime.
