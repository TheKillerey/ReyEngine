This release is mostly about the Character window. Every champion now stands on Summoner's Rift and plays its own abilities from Riot's data, and the effects follow Riot's spell and particle data. Skins also look much closer to the game: idle and persistent effects, idle variants, skin scale, and the newest skins' meshes and animations. Maps gain League's spotlights and a per-light strength. A Direct3D 11 bloom that was far too strong is toned down. On League 16.23, maps are lit again on Direct3D 11.

## Added

- **Champion simulation in the Character window.**
  - Every champion stands on Summoner's Rift (Map11) next to a target dummy.
  - P, Q, W, E and R play from the champion's own spell data, with Showcase, Replay, Pause and speed controls. Abilities can be cast back to back, and the champion returns to its idle when a clip ends.
  - Summoned units, helper spells and buff effects come from Riot's preload and luabin64 scripts.
  - A few champions whose mechanics the data cannot express are scripted:
    - Kalista: Sentinel, spear stacks, the R pull and fling.
    - Talon: Q leap and E wall vault.
    - Brand: passive stacks on the dummy.
- **Client-style picker.** Champions, skins and chromas are picked as the League client shows them, with portraits, splash cards, chroma swatches and ability icons.
- **Riot's ability video.** The Play tab shows the selected ability's poster. **Video** downloads Riot's clip on demand and opens it in your default player.
- **Spotlights.** League's `MapDynamicSpotlight` is supported:
  - load and save;
  - an outliner folder, an inspector and a viewport icon;
  - a frustum wireframe while a spotlight is selected;
  - gizmo move and rotate with undo;
  - **+ Add spotlight** in the Lighting window.

  On Direct3D 11, spotlights light the map through Riot's own spot-light code: a square frustum, linear falloff and an optional cookie texture.
- **Per-light Strength.** The Object inspector has a strength slider and box for point lights and spotlights.
  - A placed point light saves its own `intensityScale`, and nothing else in the bin changes.
  - A spotlight's strength scales its colour and keeps the hue, because the game reads only the colour.
- **One game index.** A file is found no matter which WAD holds it.

## Improved

- **Champion effects follow Riot's data:**
  - Missile bodies travel with their emitter.
  - Missiles honour start and hit bones, return legs, destroy-on-hit, arcs and accelerating flight.
  - Trails and child systems draw on Direct3D 11 and in the Particle Editor.
  - Effect shells on the champion's body use Riot's particle mesh shader.
  - Ground projections draw as decals.
  - Particles follow LTK Manager's orientation rules.
  - Effects play for their real length and land where they belong: on the caster, the aim point or the target.
- **Skins look closer to the game:**
  - A skin's persistent effects play, each with a toggle when it depends on a buff or state, e.g. Ahri's orb, Kai'Sa's glow and Aatrox's banners.
  - Effects authored inside the idle clip play, e.g. Annie's hand flame.
  - Idle variants alternate by Riot's weights. `Idle_In` plays once after loading.
  - The champion is drawn at its skin's `skinScale`.
  - A skin plays its own animation file when another skin ships one with the same name.
  - Submesh visibility carries across clips.
- **The Map11 stage costs about 4 GB instead of 13.** Changing the champion swaps only the champion.
- **`--simcheck` and `--simgen`.** ReyEngine can generate and check its own champion simulations from the command line.

## Fixed

- **League 16.23 maps were unlit on Direct3D 11.** Riot's 16.23 map shaders read a new vertex constant (`NORMAL_TANGENT_SCALE_BIAS`). ReyEngine left it at zero, so the sun, point lights and spotlights added nothing and only the lightmap showed. Maps and Content Browser thumbnails are now lit again; expect them to look brighter than in 16.23 builds before this fix.
- **The screen fog drew nothing on League 16.23.** Its shader's constant layout moved. The offsets are now read from the shader itself.
- **Newer skins no longer explode.** This affected skins whose `.skn` uses per-submesh (normalized) indices, such as K/DA FIRESTARTER.
- **The newest skins animate** instead of freezing in the bind pose. ReyEngine now reads compressed `.anm` files that leave out constant tracks.
- **Primordian Aatrox's sword and shoulder** draw their own textures on Direct3D 11.
- **Bloom on Direct3D 11 was far too strong.** The bloom levels are now averaged instead of summed. This is a calibration against an in-game screenshot, not Riot's exact formula.
- **Brand's passive** no longer wraps Brand in a flat orange shell. The marks and rings play on the dummy.
- **Maps:**
  - A map with an added mesh loads in the game again; new meshes go before the file's final mesh.
  - A material created from a shader draws in the game again.
  - Meshes can be deleted on a map with Mantis materials.

## Known limits

- **Not every ability is fully simulated.** Detonations, transforms and some displacements are still missing, and a few abilities (Ezreal P, Zilean W) are known to be incomplete.
- **Spotlights on OpenGL** show only their icon and wireframe; they do not light the map. Spotlights cast no shadows.
- **The target dummy has no skeleton,** so effect shells meant for an enemy's body are not drawn.
