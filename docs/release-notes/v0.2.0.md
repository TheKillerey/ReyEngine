The big one: **ReyEngine can now bake lighting into a League map** — including maps Riot never lightmapped at all. Plus editable dynamic lights, full old-NVR map support, and shader-macro editing.

## 💡 Light baking (M146–M168)

Bake the sun, sky, ambient occlusion and every dynamic point light into a map's lightmap atlases, using the exact light model the viewport shows. Open a map, then **☼ Lighting ▸ 🔦 Bake Lighting…**

- **Bakes into Riot's existing layout.** Atlases are written back to the very paths the mapgeo already samples, so they drop straight into the map — no bin edit, no mapgeo edit.
- **…or generates a layout from nothing.** Maps like Summoner's Rift have no lightmaps at all. **⚙ Generate Lightmap Layout** unwraps a second UV set, packs every mesh into atlas regions, rewrites the mapgeo and clears the shader macros that were blocking baked lighting. Validated on Map11: 574 meshes laid out, geometry preserved with **0.0000 bounds drift**.
- **Lightgrid** (`lightgrid.dat`) for everything a lightmap can't cover — characters, effects, and surfaces that opt out — plus the `MapBakeProperties` entry that tells the game where to find it.
- **Viewport preview**: segmented **Dynamic / Baked / Combined** modes to compare the live authoring lighting against the baked result.
- **Auto-exposure** stops low-`lightMapColorScale` maps baking to solid white. On Summoner's Rift the default exposure clipped **100%** of lit texels; auto brings that to 8%.

Formats were reverse-engineered against shipped Riot data, not guessed: `.tex` is BC3 under **format byte 12** (not the 11 the usual table claims), mips are stored **smallest-first**, and all 14 shipped `lightgrid.dat` files round-trip byte-identical.

### Quality knobs, all measured on real maps
- **Sun / point-light / AO samples.** Leaving these at 0 is what makes a bake look flat — measured, AO alone is **+57% contrast** and sun shadows **+89%**.
- **Falloff softness**, shared by the bake *and* the viewport shader from a single definition, so the two can never drift.
- **Normal smoothing** (default 120°) — map geometry is largely flat-shaded, and without this a light pool bakes as visible polygonal facets. Cuts them from 36.6% of shared edges to 1.5%.
- **Dither** on the 8-bit quantisation, so a light's faint outer tail survives as a gradient instead of snapping to a hard rim.

### Safety
Baking only writes textures. **Generating a layout rewrites geometry**, so it is gated: the rewritten mapgeo must re-parse *and* carry a usable layout or nothing is saved, Riot's originals stay untouched in the reference WAD, and the UI says so plainly. Shader macros are only cleared where the game actually ships a compiled shader permutation for the result — clearing them blindly makes the client fail with *"Unable to find correct hash for shader"*.

## 🔦 Dynamic lights are real scene objects (M152–M157)
- Point lights appear under **Map Content ▸ Lights**, are selected and edited in the Inspector, and **move with the viewport gizmo** like any mesh.
- **Per-light strength** on top of the global multiplier, and save back to Riot's `Light.dat`.
- A proper **colour picker** for light, sun and sky colours — themed to match the editor.

## 🗺️ Old NVR maps (M144–M149)
- **TGA textures**, so pre-2012 levels (Map4 and friends) load fully textured.
- **Two ground/lighting models**, chosen from the data instead of applied blindly — fixes Dominion, which v0.1.8 had flattened.
- Decals are **detected from the geometry**, fixing tiling and blending on every map.
- Maps are lit by **their own** `sun.ini` / `terrain.inibin` instead of a generic default.

## 🎛️ Materials & bins (M150–M151)
- **`shaderMacros`** are now visible and editable — `NO_BAKED_LIGHTING`, `DISABLE_DEPTH_FOG`, `PREMULTIPLIED_ALPHA`, `DISABLE_FOW`.
- The bin editor expands **Map** fields and shows **Color** and **Matrix44** values.
- **Distance fog** in the mapgeo viewport, from the map's own `MapSunProperties`.

## 🔧 Under the hood
- **A byte-exact editable mapgeo layer.** LeagueToolkit's writer downgrades v18→v17 and emits files that won't reopen, so editing a mapgeo at all needed our own. **200/200 shipped mapgeos across v13–v18 re-emit bit-identical**, and it refuses to edit anything it can't reproduce exactly.
- Baked files land at their real project-folder path instead of as opaque hashes.

---

**Install**: download the zip, extract anywhere, run `ReyEngine.App.exe`. Settings and projects carry over.

**Note on baking**: layout generation rewrites your `.mapgeo` and materials `.bin`. It's been verified through LeagueToolkit and in-game on Summoner's Rift, but work on a copy of anything you care about.
