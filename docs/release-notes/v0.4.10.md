A release about what a ported map puts on its ground.

## Fixed

- **A ported map's decals sat exactly on the ground, and everything drawn on top of them suffered for it.** The legacy porter welds its decal overlay flat onto the terrain: measured on a real port, 15,843 of its 15,845 decal vertices sit within a thousandth of a unit of the surface beneath them, where Riot's own decals on the same map sit a median of 6.4 units clear and are that flat in only 9% of cases. Two surfaces sharing one depth plane fight over it, and so does anything else drawn at ground level on top of them — which is why a champion's ground projection and a turret shot's projected sprite looked wrong over a ported map and nowhere else. The map inspector can now lift the ported decal overlay off the terrain as one undoable edit, a unit being enough. Only the porter's own decals move: the destination map's decals are placed correctly and are left where they are, as is the ported terrain itself.
